interest. The purpose of this session was to determine the Location

made to determine the point of manufacture of the equipment depic-

the document, Grill Flame Protocol, AMSAA Applied Remote Viewing 
Protocol (S), undated.

remote viewer reference his impressions of the target site. At TAB

is analyst memorandum regarding prior involvement of RVer in related 
Project 8003 sessions.
