1. (S/NOFORN) This report documents a remote viewing session conducted in 
compliance with a request from SOD, J3, OJCS, Pentagon, Washington, DC. 

The purpose of the session was to provide information relevant to the hostage 
situation in Iran. 


2. (S/NOFORN) The remote viewer's impressions of the target are provided 
as raw intelligence data, and, as such, have not been subjected to any 
intermediate analysis, evaluation or collation. Interpretation and use of 
the information provided is the responsibility of the requestor. 


3. (S/NOFORN) The protocol used for this session is detailed in the document 
Grill Flame Protocol, AMSAA Applied Remote Viewing Protocol (S), undated. 


4. (S/NOFORN) Following is a trancript of the viewer's impressions during 
the remote viewing session. At TAB A is target cuing information provided 
the remote viewer. 


5. (S/NOFORN) The remote viewer was asked to locate Robert C. Ode and 
describe his surroundings. The viewer described Ode as standing in a doorway 
with three other people nearby, two Iranian-looking males and one non~Iranian 
looking woman. 


6. (S/NOFORN) This session was cancelled prematurely when the remote viewer 
lost his ability to concentrate due to excessive heat and humidity in the 
viewing room.
