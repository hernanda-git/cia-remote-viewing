SUMMARY ANALYSIS 


REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION C 64 


1. (S) This report provides documentation of a remote viewing 
session conducted in compliance with a request from Commander, 
Intelligence Threat Analysis Center (ITAC), INSCOM, Arlington Hall 
Station, VA. This office was requested to provide information 
concerning a target located in an area designated by the geographic 
coordinate The mission for 
this initial session was to provide a physical description of the 
area perceived by the viewer as being the target. This target has 
been designated as ITAC 2B. 


2. (8) The remote viewer was highly motivated during this session, 
He was able to achieve his normal state of relaxed concentration and 
was able to resolve target area impressions. The remote viewer's 
images of an underground complex are of interest. After the session 
was completed the interviewer and remote viewer were told that the 
target of interest at the designated coordinate was, in fact, an 
underground complex. More sessions are planned against this target 
in the hope that more detailed information about this underground 
complex can be resolved. 


3. (S) The protocol used for this session is detailed in the 
document, GRILL FLAME PROTOCOL, AMSAA Applied Remote Viewing Protocol 
(S), undated. 


4. (8) Following is the transcript of the viewer's impressions 
during the remote viewing session. At TAB A are drawings made by 
the viewer reference his impressions of the target site. TAB B 
will include relevant target identifying data when it becomes 
available from ITAC.
