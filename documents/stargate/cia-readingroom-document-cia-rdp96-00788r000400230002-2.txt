SUBJECT: Summary of Unconfirmed Source Information Pertaining to the 
Area Surrounding UTM Coordinates J (S/NoForN) (cb-78) 


1. (S/NOFORN) The following information was provided by the source on 
3 July 1980_at_ 1030 hours. The information was described as pertaining to UTM 
coordinates |i—— SG1B 


a. (S/NOFORN) The source described a large radar type tower as being 
at the above coordinates. He thought that it was approximately 60 feet high 
and contained at least two whip antennae on the top, and two smaller ones on 
each side near the bottoms of the disk. He-thought there might be two other 
small disks near the top as indicated by the circles containing an "X" in 
drawing #2. He also described the antenna shown to the left of the tower in 
the drawing as being attached to the tower. He thought that the tower was 
operated by a military unit of 10 or 12 personnel for the purpose of monitoring 
shipping lanes or routes. He thought it was like a radio relay station he had 
seen in Holland. He thought that there was a black top road between the tower 
and the beach, beyond which was the shipping lanes. The source described the 
area as a warm sunny climate, and that much of the foliage was of a thick broad 
leaf variety. , 


b. (S/NOFORN) The source thought that the tower was used to monitor, 


direct or advise large cargo ships, freighters. He briefly mentioned a hidden 


or canvas covered cargo on a black Soviet ship. He seemed concerned that the 
carga was covered when its presence on the ship was "not that big a deal, its 
available for public viewing." The source thought he had seen Slavik writing 
on the bow of the black ship.. He thought the cargo was of. a military nature, 


dit"s primarily bulk cargo arms ammunition, blankets, etc., just logistical 


stuff and also some helicopters. 


c. (S/NOFORN) The source seemed at ease and confident during the 
interview. 


2, (S/NOFORN) This data should be evaluated as_soon as possible and 
comments provided to this office not later than 25 July 1980. The comments 


will be used to evaluate the accuracy and intelligence value of the data provided
