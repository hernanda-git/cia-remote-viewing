SUBJECT: DoD Program on Remote Viewing (S/ORCON) 


1. (S/ORCON) Enclosed you will find the minutes of a meeting 
held in my office on 24 August 1978, the primary intent of 
which was to initiate a coordinated DoD and Services effort 

to establish a program in remote viewing for intelligence 
purposes. Current requirements and funding are uncoordinated; 
however, a unified DoD/Services program would significantly 
improve the efficiency of the U.S, effort, reduce redundant 
research and make results available equally to the DoD and 
Services. 


2. (S/ORCON) We ask that you review the minutes and provide 

us with your comments on the proposed make-up of the 

Advisory Committee and Working Group, funding levels you 

might be able to sustain over the next five years (beginning 

in FY 79), and any general remarks you wish to make on the 
research and applied aspects of remote viewing. Please send ~~ 
your comments to my office no later than 18 September 1978. 
Subsequent to the receipt of these comments and evaluation of 
everyone's responses, we will be in touch with you, or your 
designated representative, and inform you of the time and 

date of the next meeting and proposed agenda. 


3. (U) I remind you of the extremely sensitive nature of the 
program and request that you maintain strict control of all 


documents transmitted to you.
