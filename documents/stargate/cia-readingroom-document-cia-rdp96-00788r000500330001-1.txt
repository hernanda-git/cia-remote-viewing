SUMMARY ANALYSIS 


REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION DC-68 


1. (S) This report documents a remote viewing session conducted in 
compliance with a request for information on a target of interest to 
INSCOM ITAC CRD. The purpose of this session was to describe activity 
at the target on 30 January 1981. 


2. (S) The protocol used for this session is detailed in the document, 
Grill Flame Protocol, AMSAA Applied Remote Viewing Protocol (S), undated. 


3, (S) At TAB A is a summary of the data presented by the remote viewer ° 
during the session. A complete transcript of the session is available 
upon request. At TAB B are drawings made by the remote viewer reference 
his impressions of the target site. At TABC is target cuing information 
provided the remote viewer. At TAB D are Control Analyst comments. 


4. (S) The summary at TAB A represents the salient points provided by 
the remote viewer and as such has not been subjected to any intermediate 
analysis, evaluation or collation. Interpretation and use of the infor- 
mation provided is the responsibility of the requestor.

1. (S/NOFORN) Source #07 reported the following information regarding 
the vicinity of geographic coordinates nn as obser- 
ved at 230900 hours (EST) January 1981. 


a. (S/NOFORN) A building complex with a row of towers of smoke~ 
stacks. A city is in the vicinity of the target. This city has buildings 
described as being more old than modern (style), and as being English or 
German in style. The target is on the outskirts of this town. 


b. (S/NOFORN) At the target were observed two towers connected by a 
wire or cable, and a parabolic dish shape (see erotic #5). 


2, (S/NOFORN) Source #07 reported the_following information regarding 
the vicinity of geographic coordinates fF obser- 
ved at 300900 hours (EST) January 1981. 


a. (S/NOFORN) A fence was observed located near the two towers 
which had been observed on 23 January 1981 (see sketch #5). Two aircraft 
of different types were observed (sketches #1 and #2) standing on an 
apron type area. Aircraft #2 was described as being white, large, not 
a "fighter", but not a commercial jet, and as having a "high" wing. 
Aircraft #1 was described as having blue’ stripes and insignia on its 
fuselage/empennage (see sketch #2), and as being more of a passenger 
aircraft shape. 


b. (S/NOFORN) Source also observed six jet aircraft moving in the 
area as though they were moving to parking areas (sketch #4). Source 
described the arrival of these six aircraft as appearing like "weekend 
warriors", sort of a disorganized movement in a (apparently) unfamiliar 
place. Source described the purpose of the activity of these military, 
attack bomber sized aircraft as being that of a charade or misinformation 
function, although the pilot(s) and crew(men) believed their actions/ 
activity to be just (normal) training activity. Source described the 
(apparent arrival) of these six smaller and darker aircraft as being 
associated with blowing grit, screaming jets, and also observed bunkers 
of an unknown number in the vicinity of their taxiing activity. 


c. (S/NOFORN) Also on 30 January 1981, source observed a group 
of people moving slowly, as though in a processional, in the vicinity 
of the fence, two towers, and parabolic dish. Source did not further 
describe this activity. 


d. (S/NOFORN) Source also observed, on 30 January 1981, long tubular 
shapes like those shown on sketch #3, and a large round bomb shape with 
four stabilizer fins on it's rear end reminiscent of the large bomb of 
early atomic type known as "Fat Boy."
