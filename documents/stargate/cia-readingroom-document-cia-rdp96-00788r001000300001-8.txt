REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION D-89

30 hostages in building "Metro." The viewer stated that these hostages were 
not housed at this location, but were brought here for exercise and recrea- 
tion. He described this group of hostages as being less important to 

the captors than the remaining hostages. The viewer also felt the two 

female hostages were a part of this group. He also mentioned a rear entrance/ 
exit to this building that went underground. 


2. (S/NOFORN) During debriefing the viewer mentioned that he felt this building 


was being utilized as an exercise facility 2-3 times a week, depending on 
availability of guard personnel.
