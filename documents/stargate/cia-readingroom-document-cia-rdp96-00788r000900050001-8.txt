SUMMARY ANALYSIS 


REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION C 13 


1. (S) This report provides documentation of a remote 
viewing session conducted for training purposes to enhance 
a remote viewer's demonstrated ability. 


2. (S) Target correlation seems to be apparent in this 
session. The remote viewer was slightly disturbed by ambient 
room noise which at times was quite severe. He was able to 
relax and concentrate during the periods of quiet. He relates 
that he did not develop any picture-like imagery but "felt" the 
target looked like his drawings indicate. The viewer was not 
provided the actual geographic coordinates but was told to 
focus his attention on the point represented by the coordinates 
contained in the envelope in the possession of the interviewer. 


3. (S) The protocol used for this session is detailed in 
the document, Grill Flame Protocol, AMSAA Applied Remote 
Viewing Protocol (S), undated. 


4. (S) Following is a transcript of the viewer's impressions 
during the remote viewing session. At TAB A are drawings made 
by the viewer reference his impressions of the target site. At 
TAB B is available target identifying data.
