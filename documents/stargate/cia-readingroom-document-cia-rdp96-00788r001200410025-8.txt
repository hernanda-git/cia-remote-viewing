Subj: DoD Program on Remote Viewing (S/ORCON) 
Ref: (a) DIA ltr ser S-1863/DT-1 of 6 Sep 78 


1. (S/ORCON) In reply to reference (a), I have reviewed the 
Minutes of the 24 August 1978 meeting of the DoD Committee on 
Remote Viewing. I share your concern that the current require- 
ments and funding may not be sufficiently coordinated and will 
provide support by the Office of Naval Intelligence (ONI) and 
Naval Intelligence Command (NAVINTCOM) as appropriate to better 
coordinate future efforts. 


one (S/ORCON) With regard to Navy representation on the proposed 
committees/groups, ONI/NAVINTCOM will provide representation 

as appropriate. Enclosure (1) to reference (a) was not entirely 
clear as to whether the existing DoD Committee on Remote 

Viewing would be replaced by the proposed Advisory Committee. 

I would suggest that the policy guidance be provided by a 

group comprised of the Service Deputy. ACSIs and the Working 
Group consist of highly qualified scientific advisors to 

the Service ACSI's. 


3. (S/ORCON) I am unable at this time to pledge a specific 
level of funding support. It would appear that basic 

research funds should be applied to this effort until the 
results warrant the application of advanced research resources. 
I shall attempt to identify a source of basic research funds 
with which to pursue this effort.
