SUMMARY ANALYSIS 


REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION LXI 


1. (S) This report provides documentation of a remote 
viewing session conducted for training purposes to enhance 
a remote viewer's demonstrated ability. 


2. (S) This remote viewing session was conducted without 

an interview. The remote viewer simply rendered his impres- 

sions of the target by drawing the images which came to his mind's 
eye. This remote viewer has had trouble with analytical overlay 
which may have been initiated through "left hemisphere" verbal 
communication. This session was an attempt to eliminate or 

reduce analytical overlay by eliminating the need to verbalize the 
remote viewer's impressions of the target. The remote viewer's 
drawings show some meaningful target correlation. 


3. (S) The protocol used for this session was a modification 
of the protocl detailed in the document, Standard Remote- 
Viewing Protocol (Local Targets) by Harold E. Puthoff and 
Russell Targ, November 1978. This session's protocol was 
modified in that a randomly generated preselected target was 
not used. In lieu of a randomly generated, preselected target 
the "beacon" simply chose a site which, in his opinion, was 
unique and identifiable. 


4, (S) At TAB A are drawings made by the remote viewer 


reference his impressions of the target site. At TAB B were 
photographs of the target site.
