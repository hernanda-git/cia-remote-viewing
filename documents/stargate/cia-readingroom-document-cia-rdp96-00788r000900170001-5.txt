SUMMARY ANALYSIS 


REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION C ll 


1. (S) This report provides documentation of a remote view- 
ing session conducted for training purposes to enhance a remote 
viewer's demonstrated ability. 


2. (S) Target correlation possibilities for this session are 
very small. The viewer was simply unable to locate the target. 
He appeared relaxed during the session but was unable to keep 
his mind on the task at hand. He expressed concern about 
personal problems prior to the session. Ambient room noise 
was minimal and did not disturb the viewer. The viewer's 
impression descriptions are riddled with analytic overlay. 


3. (S) The protocol used for this sessionis detailed in the 
document, Grill Flame Protocol, AMSAA Applied Remote Viewing 
Protocol (S), undated. 


4. (S) Following is a transcript of the viewer's impressions 
during the remote viewing session. At TAB A are drawings made 
by the viewer reference his impressions of the target site. 

At TAB B is available target information.
