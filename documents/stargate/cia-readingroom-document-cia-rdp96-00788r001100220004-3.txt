MEMORANDUM FOR THE DEPUTY SECRETARY OF DEFENSE 

SUBJECT: GRILL FLAME Program - ACTION MEMORANDUM (U) 

(S) The Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA) proposes contracting for tests of a 
procedure known as remote viewing. This procedure is an intellectual endeavor 
by which a person is asked to mentally perceive characteristics of a remote 
location. For example, an individual would be provided with map coordinates and 
asked to locate, identify or describe targets within the coordinates. Remote 
viewing does not involve any electronic sensing devices at or focused at the 
target site nor interpretation of photographs obtained from overhead or oblique 
means. No special equipment or environment is required for the testing and 
individuals tested will not be given drugs or other stimuli. 

(S) Approval by the Secretary or Deputy Secretary of Defense of DIA participa- 
tion in the foregoing proposal would be required by Procedure 18 if the tests 
undertaken as part of the contract or extensions of the contract or later con- 
tracts pursuant to the MOU, "may expose an individual to the possibility of 
injury (including physical, psychological, or social injury) that increases the 
ordinary risks of daily life for the subject...or that temporarily adversely 
affects a person's mental or physical condition." The DoD General Counsel has 
agreed that Procedure 18 of DoD Directive 5240.1R will be applicable to this 
contract (see enclosures) so that approval of the Secretary or Deputy Secretary 


of Defense is required.

"DoD intelligence components may not engage in or contract for

Experimentation is defined under this Procedure to include the following: 
“Experimentation in this context means a research, development, or 
related activity that may expose an individual to the possibility 
or injury (including physical, psychological, or social injury) 
that increases the ordinary risks of daily life for the subject 
(including the recogntzed risks inherent in a chosen occupation 
or field of service), or that temporarily adversely affects a 
person's mental or physical condition." 

The above procedure is an implementation of Section 2-302 of E.0. 12036. It 

is the collective view of the lawyers from DIA, DoD, and Army that approval 

should be sought prior to conducting this investigative review by contract.
