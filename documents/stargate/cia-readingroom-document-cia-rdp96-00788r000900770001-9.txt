SUMMARY ANALYSIS 


REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION XCIII 


1. (S) This report provides documentation of a remote 
viewing session conducted for training purposes to enhance 
a remote viewer's demonstrated ability. 


2. (S) The viewer's impressions during this session had some 
correlation with the geographic area of this target. The viewer 
described his position as being very high with bare earth and 
stone around him. He went on to describe rough terrain with a 
coastline to the West of his location as he was looking North. 
The target for this session was Glacier Peak (10568 feet above 
sea level) located in the Glacier Park Wilderness Area, 
approximately 60 statute miles Northeast of Seattle, Washington. 
The peak of this mountain is well above the tree line and 
therefore would be very high, barren and rocky. The coastline 
is indeed to the West. The viewer was deeply relaxed during 
this session. After the session this viewer described his con- 
fidence level as being "so-so." 


3. (S) The protocol used for this session is detailed in 
the document, Grill Flame Protocol, MASAA Applied Remote Viewing 
Protocol (S), undated. 


4. (S) Following is a transcript of the viewer's impressions 
during the remote viewing session. At TAB B is a chart and 
description of the target area.
