4. (S/SK/WNINTEL) On 16 May 1990, two demonstrations of remote 
viewing methodologies were held on behalf of four US senators in 
the Hart Senate Building. On 25 May 1990 and on 14 June 1990, 
briefings and round table discussions were held at Fort Meade on 
behalf of several congressional staffers. On 11 and 12 June 
1990, members of the Pacific Joint Task Force, JTF-5, were 
thoroughly briefed on the unit’s activity. Plans and strategies 
were formulated in preparation for the operational visit of SUN 
STREAK representatives to the Oakland and Los Angeles, California 
areas in mid-July 1990. Three remote viewers were dispatched to 
the JTF-4, Key West, Florida area for the period of 29 May to 8 
June 1990. Three additional viewers will interface with JTF-4 
analysts during the period of 25 June 1990 to i3 July 1990. 
Lastly, four remote viewers will conduct operational sessions in 
the JTF-4 area of operations from 16 July 1990 to 3 August 1990 
inclusively. During the reporting period, the unit conducted 401 
remote viewing sessions in an effort to provide a sufficient data 
base from which to evaluate the unit as a collector of 
intelligence information. In some instances, remote viewers 
conducted as many as three sessions in one day. Production in 
fact, doubled during the reporting period.
