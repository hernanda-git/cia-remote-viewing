DOD Program on Remote Viewing (S/ORCON) (Your Ltr, 6 Sep 78) 


Director, Defense Intelligence Agency/DT (Dr. Jack Vorona) 


1. (S/ORCON) We concur with your proposal to establish an 
Advisory Committee and Working Group for a DOD remote viewing 
development program. We anticipate that the Foreign Technology 
Division (FTD) will participate in the Working Group since 

it is responsible for production of threat assessment con- 
cerning Warsaw Pact paranormal research. 


2. (S/ORCON) Air Force intelligence, for the last two years, 
has supported $100K of contractual assistance for FID to 

verify data on Warsaw Pact experiments in paranormal tech- 
niques. We can and will continue to support FTD's data 
verification efforts at that level. for the foreseeable future. 
However, due to the austere level of funding within the General 
Defense Intelligence Program (GDIP), we cannot commit additional 
funds at this time. 


3.  (S/ORCON) We wish to remain cognizant of the evolving 
DOD program on remote viewing. My representative at future 
meetings and focal point for this subject will be Lt Colonel 
Michael Bloom, INYSA, ext. 51357.
