TAGPC-G 24 March 1982 


MEMORANDUM FOR ADCSOPS-HUMINT 
SUBJECT: Purchase Request (U) 


1. (U) The attached DF requests the purchase of the following: 


a. (U) Three Cassette Tape Decks, AIWA AD-R500U. The decks are available 
at Audio Associates, 929 Fairlawn Avenue, Laurel, MD. The price is $420.00 each, 
plus tax. 


b. (U) One Stereo Audio Mixer which is also available at Audio Associates 
in Laurel. The price is $200.00, plus tax. 


2. (S/NOFORN) JUSTIFICATION: 


a. (U) The tape recorders on hand no longer provide adequate reliability or 
versatility to support our mission. The on-hand records were purchased in 1979, 
as temporary units. They are inexpensive (2@$46.00 and 20$69.95), low quality 
cassette recorders designed only for occasional home use. This office has con- 
ducted approximately one hour per day of recording since 1979. This rate of 
recording will increase in the future. As the hours of recording have increased, 
equipment malfunctions have become more prevelant. Reliability of recording 
equipment is essential to accurate transcription and eventual reporting of 
intelligence collected by this office. The cost of repairing on-hand equipment 
would exceed the replacement cost of like items. What is needed is better equipment 
that will serve our needs now and in the future. On-hand recorders can continue 
to be used during training and for reviewing for accuracy previous session trans- 
cripts. 


b. (S/NOFORN) The Aiwa AD R500U Cassette Decks are required because of their 
superior construction and reliability. They are designed as “work-horse" units 
for long life and durability. Their metal tape capability and bi-directional 
operation are unique features. Metal tape will enable permanent record and high 
use rate (daily training) of the Monroe Training which is being implemented. 
(See Appendix A with Inclosures.) The Monroe Training tapes will be copied on a 
metal tape format thereby extending their useful life (an actual dollar savings 
in the long run). Bi-directional operation will allow for continuous recording 
of remote viewing sessions eliminating the interruption of the RVer by stopping 
the session to turn over the tape. Also bi-directional operation will allow for 
the continuous playback of Monroe training tape while in use in this office.

c. (S/NOFORN) The cassette decks will also be used for the recording of 
biological monitoring data simultaneous with the viewer's impressions of the 
target. Biophysical monitoring data will be used to determine the accuracy/ 
reliability of the viewer's impressions. This office has submitted changes/ 
additions to the INSCOM GRILL FLAME Protocol concerning the use of non-intrusive 
biophysical monitoring devices. (See Appendix B with Inclosures.) 


d. (S/NOFORN) The NUMARK DM-1000 Audio Mixer is required for use in con- 
junction with the above cassette decks and their application to the Monroe 
training. The DM-1000 mixer is a heavy duty piece of equipment with the capability 
of mixing the audio sources of the above recorders with the voice of the trainer/ 
interviewer. By mixing trainer/interviewer with the Monroe cassettes the program 
can be tailored to the specific training requirements of the RVer and the opera- 


tional requirements of specific missions. 


e. (U) The above equipment has been specifically selected as mission 
required. It will fulfill present and anticipated needs for the near term. No 
substitute of items listed is authorized as this office has conducted extensive 
research and determined that these are the only acceptable items for mission 
accomplishment. IMDSO or ICF purchase and IBP of items is recommended. Request 
must go through DCSLOG for IBP items. Prior coordination with supply has been 
affected, POC is Mr. Hamilton. IMDSO wants brand names and local POC for 
purchase.
