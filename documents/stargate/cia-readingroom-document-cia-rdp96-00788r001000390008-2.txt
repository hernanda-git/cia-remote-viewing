3. (S/SK/WNINTEL) [7] completed Stage III training of 
the Coordinate Remote Viewing (CRV) methodology during the last 
week of June 1988. ompleted training in the 
Extended Remote Viewing (E “methodology in early June 1988. 
will start CRV training during the week of 11 July 
1988. WRV will continue to be utilized extensively in an effort 
to determine its effectiveness while employed in a search mode 
and in a future mode. Projected dates of release affecting the 
hostages have been repeatedly delayed and rescheduled. The most 
recent indicators predict that the release of the hostages has 
heen postponed by another five to six weeks. Events as remote- 
viewed in future time, seem to be subject to many elements that 
skew the accuracy of the prediction. The greater the time span 
addressed and the more involved the players and the events, the 


greater the room for error. In any case, information provided to. 


date appears consistent with other intelligence findings; 

therefore, WRV will provide weekly updates on the continuing 
expectations regarding the release of the hostages until such 
time as their actual demise is confirmed by credible sources.
