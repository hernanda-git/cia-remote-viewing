SUMMARY ANALYSIS 


REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION LXXV 


1. (S) This report provides documentation of a remote 
viewing session conducted for training purposes to enhance 
a remote viewer's demonstrated ability. 


2. (S) The remote viewer's impressions during this session 
seem to correlate well with the geographic area selected as 
this target. The viewer's descriptions included mountains, 

a body of water, "pup" tents and a feeling of being ina 
remote location. The target, Yellowstone National Park, is 
located on a high plateau, (8500 feet above sea level), 
surrounded by mountains. Approximately 15 miles East of the 
target coordinates is Yellowstone Lake, a large body of water. 
This entire area of Yellowstone National Park is a popular 
tourist attraction and camping area. To the South of this 
target is the Teton Wilderness Area. The viewer appeared calm, 
relaxed and confident during the session. During a post- 
interview discussion, the viewer stated that he felt tired 
and drained of energy after completion of this session, his 
first attempt at geographic coordinate remote viewing. 


3. (S) The protocol used for this session is detailed in 
the document, Grill Flame Protocol, AMSAA Applied Remote 
Viewing Protocol (S), undated. 


4. (S) Following is a transcript of the viewer's impressions 
during the remote viewing session. At TAB A are drawings made 
by the viewer reference his impressions of the target area. 

At TAB B is a description and map of the target area.
