REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION DC-74 


1. (S/NOFORN) Source #27.5 reported the following information regarding the 
vicinity of geographic coordinates as observed 
at 1200 hours, 31 January 1981 (time at the target). SGIA 


a. (S/NOFORN) The target is located on the edge of a farming or light 
industrial town, an "old fashioned" city. In this area are dingy warehouses 
with windows broken out. The area appears "backward" and "behind the times", 
like the 1930's. 


b. (S/NOFORN) Elsewhere near the target is open rolling ground with a 
lot of dirt roads. The general terrain is that of a leveled area of land in 
the midst of foothills. (Sketches #1, #2) 


c. (S/NOFORN) Several trucks (sketch #5) were observed in the vicinity 
of the target. These trucks were described variously as being like an old 
"wrecker" type truck with "pulley type wheels" on the back, and as being 
like an “old fashioned milk truck", but with fuel (instead of milk) and an 
apparently funny kind of off-set cab. The trucks were colored ceo or 
rust. 


d. (S/NOFORN) These vehicles "pick-up" their loads at a location near 
the target area having a "T" shaped building (sketch #3) serviced by a 
vehicular ramp. Within the overhang area of the "T" shaped building is an area 
of glassed-in rooms (sketch #4) from which the movement and “comings and 
going" activity can be observed/monitored. This area was described as a 
"traffic" center. 


2, (S/NOFORN) The following was reported regarding geographic coordinates 
SGI1A as observed at 1200 hours, 7 February 1981. 


a. (S/NOFORN) Within the "traffic center", personnel observing the 
vehicular traffic would provide a central figure little chits of paper. 
Data pertaining to these chits of paper would then be transcribed on a clip- 
board maintained by the central figure. 


b. (S/NOFORN) Accompanying the central figure was a man with field 
glasses described as the other's "right hand man". Using the field glasses, 
this man was observing the progress of a convoy some distance away, which 
was going around the edge of a mountain. (See sketches #1 and #2) 


c. (S/NOFORN) The man with the field glasses was described as reminding 
the observer of a "major". The situation on 7 February 1981 was one of the 
"major" watching increased activity. This was an abnormal situation of higher 
than normal activity during a "peak operations period", The source stated the 
activity involved was "definitely military".
