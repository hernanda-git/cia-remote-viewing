SUMMARY ANALYSIS 


REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION C 


1. (S) This report provides documentation of a remote 
viewing session conducted for the purpose of orienting a 
remote viewer to an applied remote viewing protocol. 


2. (S) The remote viewer's impressions during this session 
appear to correlate well with the selected target. The viewer 
seemed to be having some difficulty in obtaining any imagery 
during this session. It was eleven minutes after the beginning 
of the session that the viewer received the first impressions. 
The viewer had an impression of what looked like a very long 
tunnel. The viewer seemed somewhat confused by this image, 
because at times it appeared arched and at other times it 
appeared square. The target selected for this session was an 
above ground pipeline. When you look at the on-site photographs 
(TAB B), you can plainly see the square, long tunnel in the upper 
left photograph. The long arched tunnel is apparent in the upper 
right photograph. Although the viewer's impressions were brief 
and sketchy, they appear to be very accurate. The viewer was 
relaxed and was not disturbed by ambient room noise. However, 
this viewer suggested that a darker room might enhance the 

remote viewing process. 


3. (S) The protocol used for this session and which will 

be used in later training is detailed in the document, Standard 
Remote Viewing Protocol (Local Targets) by Harold E. Puthoff and 
Russell Targ, November 1978. 


4. (S) Following is a transcript of the viewer's impressions 
during the remote viewing session. At TAB A are drawings made 
by the viewer reference his impressions of the target site. At 
TAB B are photographs of the target site.
