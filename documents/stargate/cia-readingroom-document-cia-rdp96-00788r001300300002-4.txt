Addendum 


Initially it was planned that by the end of fiscal year 1982 a 


demonstration of the applicability of remote viewing to intelligence 


problems would be held, The following fiscal year, 1983, would be concerned 


with solving any additional problems and integrating the remote viewing 
ability into the defense intelligence arena. Despite the setbacks, thus 
far, the original goals and dates have not been altered, However, upon 
review of the work done and work to be done, it appears that, because of 
the setbacks, it would not be in the best interest of the program to 
adhere to the dates, milestones, and goals presently set. Therefore, 
instead of holding the demonstration of remote viewing and its value to 
the intelligence community at the end of FY 1982 it is rescheduled for 
mid-year of FY 1983, This additional time will allow many of the diffi- 
culties imposed by the funding problems to be corrected. None of the 


other tasks in the Statement of Work will be altered. 


This move has been coordinated with SRI International (Dr. Puthoff) 


and he is in full agreement with the change,
