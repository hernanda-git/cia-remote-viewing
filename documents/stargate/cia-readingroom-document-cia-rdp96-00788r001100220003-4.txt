MEMORANDUM FOR THE DEPUTY SECRETARY OF DEFENSE 
SUBJECT: GRILL FLAME Program - ACTION MEMORANDUM (U) 


(S) The Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA) proposes contracting for tests of 

a procedure known as remote viewing. This procedure is an intellectual endeavor 
by which a person is asked to mentally perceive characteristics of a remote 
location. For example, an individual would be provided with map coordinates 

and asked to locate, identify or describe targets within the coordinates. 

Remote viewing does not involve any electronic sensing devices at or focused 

at the target site nor interpretation of photographs obtained from overhead 

or oblique means. No special equipment or environment is required for the 
testing and individuals tested will not be given drugs or other stimuli. 


(S) Approval by the Secretary or Deputy Secretary of Defense of DIA partici- 
pation in the foregoing proposal would be required by Procedure 18 if the tests 
undertaken as part of the contract or extensions of the contract or later 
contracts pursuant to the MOU, "may expose an individual to the possibility 

of injury (including physical, psychological, or social injury) that increases 
the ordinary risks of daily life for the subject...or that temporarily adversely 
affects a person's mental or physical condition." The DoD General Counsel has 
agreed that Procedure 18 of DoD Directive 5240.1R will be applicable to this 
contract (see enclosures) so that approval of the Secretary or Deputy Secretary 
of Defense is required. 


(S) This contract is a joint service (DIA and Army) effort known as GRILL FLAME 
to determine the operational parameters and usefulness of the phenomenon of 
psychoenergetics. This term, psychoenergetics, includes both the programs 
discussed below. The total program would be jointly funded by DIA and Army. 
This program will include contracting for tests to examine remote viewing 
(clairvoyance) and psychokinetics (remote perturbation). Psychokinetics is 

the ability to move inanimate objects from a distance; many examples can be 
hypothesized. 


(S) The proposal merits your approval in order to assess the practicality in 
applying paranormal functions to military and intelligence goals. Further, 

the ‘research would determine if functions can be utilized by intelligence analysts 
to enhance their grasp, understanding and knowledge of areas of military interest 
through application of psychoenergetic techniques to military targets. Most 
importantly, it would permit the Intelligence Community to assess the threat 

such phenomena might pose to the national security and means by which non- 
friendly applications could be sensed and negated.
