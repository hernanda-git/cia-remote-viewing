SUMMARY ANALYSIS 


REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION C9 


1. (S) This report provides documentation of a remote view- 
ing session conducted for training purposes to enhance a remote 
viewer's demonstrated ability. 


2. (S) This session provided for some target correlation. 

For the first portion of the "on target" (OT) time the "beacon" 
individual sat near the fountain pool behind the First Army 
Headquarters building. The "beacon" later walked around the 
building during the remainder of the OT time. The viewer seems 
to have focused on the fountain pool area. The session was 
conducted after normal duty hours and ambient room noise was 
therefore zero. The viewer was able to relax completely and 
focus on the target. The viewer was anxious about this session 
and satisfied with the results. This was this viewer's final 
session prior to going to SRI International. 


3. (S) The protocol used for this session is detailed in 
the document, Standard Remote Viewing Protocol (Local Targets) 
by Harold E. Puthoff and Russell Targ, November 1978. 


4. (S) Following is a transcript of the viewer's impressions 
during the remote viewing session. At TAB A are drawings made 
by the viewer reference his impressions of the target site. 

At TAB B is a photograph of the target site.
