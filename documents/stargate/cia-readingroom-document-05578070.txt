(U//A¥6@) Interest Is on the Increase at NARA for CIA's 25-Year Product 

(U// 4303 Working level personnel at the National Archives and Records Administration (NARA) report that 
public demand for the CIA material on the CIA Records Search Tool (CREST) system has increased significantly 
during the last two weeks as many new researchers have requested access to the CREST database. 


@ CREST is a database of CIA declassified records available to the public on terminals located at NARA in 
College Park, Maryland. The CREST system currently contains about 3 million declassified pages; an 
additional 2 million pages processesd.in FY 2001 are awaiting delivery. 


(U//ATOO) From the Archives: Sampling of DCI Correspondence from 1958 
(U//AtS@% The DCI team at the CIA Declassification Center currently is reviewing correspondence from the Allen 
Dulles era. Following is a sampling from 1958 that gives a general flavor of the types of correspondence generated 
by this high level public official. 

eA letter turning down an offer by a film organization that wanted to do a film “utilizing the files of the CIA, 

that have dramatic merit and reflect favorably upon your agency;” 

e A note to the head of the Far East Division thanking him for his hard work and sending best wishes on his 
new overseas posting; 
A thank you note to an author who had sent a copy of his book on the Mideast; 
A thank you note to a member of his security staff who was transferring; 
Several letters to lawyers and legal societies expressing interest in their ideas and activities; 
A letter of condolence to a former colleague's widow and another to a staffer whose young daughter had 
contracted tuberculosis in an overseas assignment, 
A few job recommendations for staffers departing the Agency and others whom he was interested in hiring; 
Numerous "social/business related" thank yous for dinners; 
A note to John Foster Dulles, his brother who at the time was Secretary of State; 
A note to John McCone congratulating him on his appointment to head the Atomic Energy Commission 
(McCone would replace Allen Dulles as DCI in 1961;) 
e =A request to the Baltimore Sun for a copy of a cartoon from their newspaper.
