REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION XCII 


1. (S) This report provides documentation of a remote viewing 
session conducted for training purposes to enhance a remote 
viewer's demonstrated ability. 


2. (S) The remote viewer's impressions during this session 

contained several good correlations to the randomly selected 

target. Throughout the session, the viewer was convinced that 
SG1A he was located somewhere along the coast of China. The actual 


Another interesting impression that 
the viewer had was one of darkness. He mentioned several times 
that it was very dark and he was having trouble "seeing" things. 
At the time this session had begun, it was 8:30 PM in the area 
of the target location. This viewer remained in a deep state 
of relaxation during the entire session. Ambient room noise 
is often a problem for this viewer. However, it did not seem 
to bother him during this session. 


3. (S) The protocol used for this session is detailed in 
the document, Grill Flame Protocol, AMSAA Applied Remote 
Viewing Protocol (S), undated.

during the remote viewing session. At TAB A are drawings made 
by the viewer reference his impressions of the target site. 

At TAB B is a description and map of the target location.
