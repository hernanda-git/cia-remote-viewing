REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION CC 94 


1. (S) This report documents a remote viewing session conducted 
in compliance with a request for information on a target of interest.

target area at the time of the session. Ambient room noise was moderate 
and only slightly disturbed the viewer. The viewer did not appear 

as physically relaxed as usual. He was worried about his impressions 

of the target and was reluctant to verbalize because he thought he 

may be fantasizing about the target.

document, Grill Flame Protocol, AMSAA Applied Remote Viewing Protocol
