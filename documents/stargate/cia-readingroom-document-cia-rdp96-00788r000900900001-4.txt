SUMMARY ANALYSIS 


REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION LXXVIII 


1. (S) This report provides documentation of a remote 
viewing session conducted for training purposes to enhance 
a remote viewer's demonstrated ability. 


2. (S) The remote viewer's impressions during this session 
had little correlation with the target location. The viewer 
was relaxed, cheerful and in a positive frame of mind prior 
to the session. During the session the viewer seemed to 

have some difficulty describing what he was "seeing" at times. 
At the conclusion of this session, the viewer stated that he 
did not have a feeling, one way or another, whether this 
might be a good session or a bad one. 


3. (S) The protocol used for this session is detailed in 
the document, Standard Remote Viewing Protocol (Local Targets) 
by Harold E. Puthoff and Russell Targ, November 1978. 


4. (S) Following is a transcript of the viewer's impressions 
during the remote viewing session. At TAB A are drawings 

made by the viewer reference his impressions of the target 
site. At TAB B are photographs of the target site.
