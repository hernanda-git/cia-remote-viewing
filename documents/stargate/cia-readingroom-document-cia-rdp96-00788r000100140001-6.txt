SUMMARY ANALYSIS 
REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION C77 


1. (8S) This report provides documentation of a remote viewing 
session conducted for training purposes to enhance a remote 
viewer's demonstrated ability. 


2. (8) During a previous session this viewer was tasked to look 
at an area and describe the location, activity and event to take 
place. The remote viewer described an area located adjacent to 
the target area. During this session the viewer was asked to go 
back to the area he described previously and was directed to 
describe the area adjacent to him. His description of this target 
area correlated well with the type of activity known to take place 
at the target location, 


3. (8) The protocol used for this session is detailed in the 
document, Grill Flame Protocol, AMSAA Applied Remote Viewing 
Protocol (8), undated, 


4. (8) Following is a transcript of the viewer's impressions 


during the remote viewing session. At TAB A are drawings made by the 
viewer reference his impressions of the target site.
