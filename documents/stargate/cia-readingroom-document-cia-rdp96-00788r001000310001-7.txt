TRANSCRIPT SUMMARY 


REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION D-91 


1. (S/NOFORN) The remote viewer reported that he perceived three americans 
(at least) in a large room on the ground floor within the target. building. 
He felt that these three were having a meeting which they had regularly 

to keep their spirits up. The viewer felt there was one other hostage 

who did not attend this meeting and was elsewhere in the target building 
doing some reading. 


2. (S/NOFORN) The viewer was unable to recognise or identify any of the hostages 
he saw. He seemed to have very little visual sensation of the target but 

did seem to "tune into " the emotional content of the target building. The 
viewer felt that the hostages he saw were drained of any identity. He 

felt it was as though they no longer had any individual egos. The viewer 

also felt that the hostages did not feel any hostility towards their captors. 
The hostages seemed to have given up all hope. 


3. (S/NOFORN) The viewer perceived three guards associated with the target. 
building. Two of these guards were in the room where the hostages were having 
their meeting. The whereabouts of the other guard was not reported. The 
guards seemed bored and appeared to be wishing that this affair was over. The 
guards seemed to be on a "first name basis" with the hostages. No weapons 
were reported. 


4. (S/NOFORN) At one point in the session the viewer thought that the hostages 
might have inadvertantly received some news (subject unknown) which made them 


excited. The emotional state of the hostages concerning this news was described 
as excited but not daring to be hopefull.
