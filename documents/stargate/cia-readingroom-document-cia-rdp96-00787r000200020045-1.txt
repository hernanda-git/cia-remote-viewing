September 23, 1977 


Hal Puthoff and Russ Targ 
SRI 
Menlo Park, CA 94025 


Dear Hal and Russ, 


I finally received a reply from Senator Proxmire which seems 
rather encouraging. I hope that you will still have an opportunity 
to use this with your potential funding sources. 


He never responded to the original letter which I sent him 
several months ago. Perhaps it was too complicated or aggressive. 
Recently I sent another letter. I don't have a copy of the 
original, but I am sending you a copy of the rough draft on which 
my letter to the Senator was based. The final copy is just a bit 
more polished. Although the second letter doesn't mention your 
work as specifically as the first letter, I did send him a copy of 
the UC Extension brochure for the summer program which lists both 
of you as lecturers--as well as Ed and Elizabeth. 


I believe that I will complete the initial statistical analysis 
of my remote-viewing data sometime in the next week. Hope and 
Elizabeth have now finished the judging. It's clear to me that 
at different times during the training program everyone in my group 
exhibited greater than 90% accuracy in remote viewing--with perhaps 
one or two exceptions. Often the most striking instances were not 
during the formal experiments. I hope to have the complete 
dissertation finished by mid November. 


Best of luck in your own work. I hope that we can work 
together on some future projects. (Today I am meeting with the 
president of an oil company to analyze some pilot remote-viewing 
experiments on four of his oil fields.) 


/- cerely, 
y
