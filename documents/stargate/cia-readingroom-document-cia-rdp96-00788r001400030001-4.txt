SUMMARY ANALYSIS 
REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION #847 


1. (S/NOFORN) This report provides documentation of a remote viewing 
session conducted for training purposes only. 


2. (S/NOFORN) Post analysis on the target viewing indicated some degree 
of correlation with the viewers impressions of the area. Some of the 
correlation was quite remarkable at this stage of training. The sig- 
nificant items identifiable to the target were almost all noted by the 
viewer. Even though he did not specifically call a pool in the aquarium 
an aquarium pool, he did produce all of the significant features ident- 
ifiable to an aqaurium pool. 


3. (S/NOFORN) The protocol used for this session is detailed in the do- 
cument Standard Remote-Viewing Protocol (Remote Target) by Harold E. 
Putthoff and Russel Targ, November 1978.

STATEMENT 


REMOTE VIEWING SESSION #847 


1. (U) A transcript for Session #847 is not available due to a 
malfunction with the tape recording device. The malfunction was 
not noticed till some time following termination of the session 
and therefore is non-correctable. 


2. (S/NOFORN) Viewer described some sort of a structure constructed 
from a framework of wood and aluminum. He described slabs of concrete 

a large lake type body of water which appeared to be elevated above 

the ground level. He described a small dam, but the sides kept changing 
from concave to convex lines. He stated that there was a falsh of a road 
sign "Interstate #41". He reported small roads, canals, trees, pools of 
water, and a great body of water on the horizon. All water areas were 
manmade and elevated, but he could not decide if they were of concrete or 
not. He reported a small water fall and maintenance type personnel who 
were concerned with water levels and reading control type instruments. 
Dirt mounds were also reported. 


3, (S/NOFORN) The actual target was the Miami Seaquarium on Rickenbacker 
Causeway and the bay. Correlation is clearly represented by the diagrams 
and pictures in the seaquarium handout.
