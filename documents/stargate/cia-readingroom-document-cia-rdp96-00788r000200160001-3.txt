SUMMARY ANALYSIS 


REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION CC 98 


1. (S) This session documents a remote viewing session conducted 
in compliance with a request for information on a target of interest. 


2. (S) At the time of the session the remote viewer was provided 

with the geographic coordinates of the target area and asked to 

orient himself to the target based on his descriptions from a previous 
session, CC96. The viewer was unable to attain his usual state of 
relaxed concentration. He was troubled by car problems which came upon 
him on his way to work. He did not have confidence in the imagery 

he did resolve. He was troubled by ambient room noise. 


3. (S) The protocol used for this session is detailed in the document 
Grill Flame Protocol, AMSAA Applied Remote Viewing Protocol (S), undated. 


4. (S) Following is a transcript of the viewer's impressions during 


the remote viewing session. At TAB A are drawings made by the viewer 
reference his impressions of the target site.
