Subject: Interview with “Mr. Patrick H. Price on 26 April 1975. 


Administrative 


For the record, the following administrative points were 
covered with Pat on 26 April 1975 during our meeting at the 
Key Bridge Marriott Motel: 


Our relationship is now an operational one. We are no 
longer conducting R & D. He was very pleased with this and 
eager to go to work. 


He was asked what conditions help him to work at his 

_ best? Does it help to see the person or a photograph? Can 
he really get there beat by grid coordinates, etc.? He 
feels we must provide more information so that he can make a 
positive identification of a site. Grid coordinates are ok 
to establish the general area of interest but then he must 
be told what specific point to zero in on. Photographs are 
very good, particularly when a person or a building is in- 
volved. More data is needed to help pinpoint a location in 
relation to a known landmark such as a city or a topographic 


feature. If the point is within a city then provide a locator 


point such as a building description.

of "compartmentation" and "need to know" will be SOP. Also

played up in the press. He agreed to keep me posted on other
