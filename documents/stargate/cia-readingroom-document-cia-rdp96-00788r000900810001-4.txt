SUMMARY ANALYSIS 


REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION XIV 


1. (8S) This report provides documentation of a remote 
viewing session conducted for the purpose of orienting 

a remote viewer to the protocol which will be used in 
the remote viewing training program at Stanford Research 
Institute (SRI), Menlo Park, California. 


2. (8S) The remote viewer was frustrated during this 
session. The ambient room noise was exceedingly bad dur- 
ing this session. This noise factor seemed to distract 
the remote viewer significantly. He was unable to 
concentrate on the task at hand and after trying for 
fifteen minutes, called off the session. 


3. (8S) The protocol used for this session and which will 
be used in later training is detailed in the document, 
Standard Remote-Viewing Protocol (Local Targets) by Harold 
E. Puthoff and Russell Targ, November 1978. 


4. (S) Following is a transcript of the viewer's 
impressions during the remote viewing session. At TAB A 
are drawings made by the viewer reference his impressions 
of the target site. At TAB B are photographs of the target 
site. At TAB C is a post-session interview.

POST-SESSION INTERVIEW 


REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION XIV 


1. (S) Post-session interviews are conducted after the 
completion of a session (usually the following day) to 
provide the selected remote viewer with the opportunity 
to express himself concerning his viewing experience. 


2. (S) #10.5 was very depressed after calling off the 
session. He had been looking forward to being very 
successful and having a rewarding experience. #10.5 

had one target image which showed possible target cor- 
relation but this was of little comfort to him. Project 
personnel are not discouraged by this session as it 
served as a valuable learning tool for #10.5. #10.5 
remains enthusiastic about remote viewing and is eager 

to conduct another session. He also harbors an acute 
need to have a rewarding remote viewing experience. #10.5 
is therefore, seeking a high degree of target correlation. 


3. (8S) The relationship between #10.5 and #7.5 remains 
stable. #7.5 was very empathetic towards the feelings 

of #10.5. #7.5 and #10.5 have a mutually supportive 
relationship which will serve to overcome present problems.
