SUMMARY ANALYSIS 


REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION CD-08 


1. (S) This report documents a remote viewing session conducted in 
compliance with a request for information on a target of interest. 


2. (S) The remote viewer appeared to be having a very difficult time 
concentrating on the target. He stated that ambient room noise was 
bothering him considerably before, and during this session. The viewer 
had very little confidence in his impressions. 


3. (S) The protocol used for this session is detailed in the document, 
Grill Flame, Protocol, AMSAA Applied Remote Viewing Protocol (S), undated. 


4. (S) Following is a transcript of the viewer's impressions during the 
remote viewing session. At TAB A are drawings made by the viewer reference his 
impressions of the target site. At TAB is target cuing information provided 
the remote viewer at the time of this session
