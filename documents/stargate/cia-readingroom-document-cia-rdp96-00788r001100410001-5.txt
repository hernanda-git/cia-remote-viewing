Classified Statement of Work: Psychoenergetics -- 
Threat to US and Potential Applications (FY84) 
1.0 General (U) 
(S/NOFORN) During FY 83, remote viewing received the greatest attention 
and it was shown to be an excellent method for obtaining information on a 
distant site. It has excellent potential for gathering intelligence on 
sites that are denied to more conventional collection techniques. In 
FY84, experiments will confirm’ or deny its infusion into the US 
intelligence gathering apparatus. The emphasis for FY 84 will be on both 
improving its reliability and demonstrating its trainability. A major 
effort will also be expended on expanding its scope from using coordinates 
to target the remote viewer to a system where a variety of targeting 
methods can be used. In addition to the existing and developing training 
program, new methods and approaches to training will be examined. New 


methods of evaluating results from remote viewing will be sought. 


(S/NOFORN) The psychokinesis (PK) area will receive considerable attention 
in FY 84. The emphasis will be on those areas that may be adapted to 
countermeasures. Both preventive and detecting methods are to be 


examined.

2.0 Specific Tasks (FY 84) (U) 
2.1 (S) Research and develop the final stages of remote viewing. 
2.1.1 Expand remote viewing ability to include both configural and 
analytical capability. 
2.1.2 Demonstrate its trainability in the latter stages. 
2.1.3 Perform quarterly experiments that show training improves remote 
viewing performance. 
2.1.3 Demonstrate an accuracy of at least 70% in the remote viewing 


derived data.
