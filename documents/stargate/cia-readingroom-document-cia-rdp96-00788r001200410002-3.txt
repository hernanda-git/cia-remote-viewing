The need to establish a full-time, dedicated effort is evidenced by the following: 


(1) People cannot properly function working at two locations and being 
responsible for responding to requirements from different offices. 


(2) Intelligence analysts need to be assigned full-time to the task force 
in order to provide necessary interface with analysts from outside agencies and to 
give necessary, timely direction to project personnel as mission requirements develop. 


(3) Two full-time secretaries are required to support this project. Each 
report produced requires 4-6 hours of typing support. Right now, the program's 
production ability is severely hampered by lack of necessary administrative support. 


(4) Remote viewers must be available to respond to tasking requirements 
and not off on TDY, etc., in conjunction with another job. 


(5) Analysts need to develop understanding and familiarization with remote 
viewing and associated phenomena. 


e. In order to reduce manpower requirements, several members of the task force 
will combine duties as remote viewers with their assigned job within the task force. 


£. Establishment of this task force will not eliminate the need for continued 
part-time support from other individuals already identified as having possible remote 
viewing capabilities.
