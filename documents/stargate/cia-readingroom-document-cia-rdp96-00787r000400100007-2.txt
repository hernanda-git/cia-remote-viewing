SRI BRIEFING: RESEARCH ON PARANORMAL PHENOMENA 
September 24, 1974 


Harold Puthoff and Russell Targ 


1. Introduction and description of early SRI experiments involving 
the perturbation of a superconducting magnetometer. Results 
indicate both psychokinetic and paranormal perceptual ability 


of the subject, Ingo Swann. 


2. Techniques to enhance man/machine communication. Experiments 
with a four-state random stimulus generator used as an ESP 


teaching machine. 


3. Remote viewing. Experimental evidence for direct perception of 


remote geographical locations. 


4. Information transmission under conditions of sensory shielding. 


Picture-drawing experiments with Uri Geller. 


5. Experimental factors related to paranormal perception in a 


uniform-sensory setting (ganzfeld procedure); film. 
6. Arousal response in EEG with the presentation of remote stimuli. 
7. Physics, entropy and psychokinesis, a summary of recent experiments. 


8. Real time Kirlian video photography high-voltage corona discharge 


experiments.
