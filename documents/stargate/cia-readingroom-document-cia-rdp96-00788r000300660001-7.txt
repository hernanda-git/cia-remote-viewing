SUMMARY ANALYSIS 


REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION CD41 


1. (S) This report documents a remote viewing session conducted in 
compliance with a request for information on a target of interest to USI. 
The purpose of this session was to describe a target building and deter- 
mine the activity therein for a specified date and time. 


2. (S) The protocol used for this session is detailed in the document, 
Grill Flame Protocol, AMSAA Applied Remote Viewing Protocol (S), undated. 


3. (S) At TAB A is a summary of the data presented by the remote viewer 
during the session. A complete transcript of the session is available upon 
request. At TAB B are drawings made by the remote viewer reference his 
impressions of the target site. At TAB C is target cuing information pro- 
vided the remote viewer. 


4. (S) The summary at TAB A represents the salient points provided by the 
remote viewer and as such has not been subjected to any intermediate analysis, 
evaluation or collation. Interpretation and use of the information provided 
is the responsibility of the requestor.

1. (S) The following information was provided for 1100 hours vice 1000 
hours which would normally be the correct time at which the talks would 
begin. This was done to insure the source would provide predicted .infor- 
mation pertinent to the talks while ongoing and not just prior to them 
occuring. 


a. (S) Source stated that his target was a large wooden, very white, 
building, with a red roof, 


b. (S) Source stated that the roof on this building was covered with 
what appeared to be a "tin" type of material. 


c. (S) He stated that the interior of the building was empty and there 
didnt appear to be any ongoing activity, nor would there be any activity on 
the 24th of June, at 1100 hours. He described what would appear to be some 
sort of empty stalls (walled partitions) lining both sides of the buildings 
interior. 


d. (S) The source stated that there was also a truck parked in the 
vicinity of the building. 


e. (S) Source didn't feel that he was given the right coordinates for 
the target, because he stated that it area looked like a large old farm. 


2. (S) Request the above information be evaluated and comments be provided 
to the undersigned in writing no later than 27 June 1980, this will permit 

a full evaluation of the source of information. for both accuracy and intell-
