Additions/Changes to the proposed Statement of Work for FY82 (U) 


(S/NOFORN) 2.4.0 Continue development and evaluation of the Coordinate 
Remote Viewing (CRV) Training Program. Train Army 
personnel in the CRV technique. 


(S/NOFORN) 2.4.1 The CRV Training Program is to be developed in phases. 
This will allow Army personnel to be trained after the 
development and evaluation of the individual phases 

_and prior to the completion of the entire CRV Training 
Program. 


(S/NOFORN) 2.4.2 Army personnel to be trained will be jointly selected 
by INSCOM and SRI. After the completion of each 
training phase, SRI personnel involved in the development 
program will have reasonable access to Army personnel 
trained to assist in further evaluation of the program.
