2. (S/SK/WNINTEL) First Quarter Training Highlights (U) 


a. (S/SK/WNINTEL) Sources #003, #018, #079, and #101 were 
dropped from ARDBA training during this quarter. Both source #011 
and #021 continue to display a greater-than-statistically-average 
ARDBA performance. ARDBA session scoring criteria and individual 
source evaluations are available for review in the unit training 
files. 


b. (S/SK/WNINTEL) The integration of both source #011 and #079 
into operations began during the last week of the quarter. Source 
#079 completed ERV training on 13 March. Performance over 21 ERV 
training sessions indicate that the source experienced a breakthrough 
towards mid-quarter, after which achievement levels remained 
generally high, (i.e., excellent target-related data correspondence). 
Source #011 completed CRV Stage 4 training on 26 March and is 
scheduled to begin Stage 5 training during the second week of April 
87. Performance over 29 CRV training sessions portray a steadily 
increasing skill level, with the concept of site “intangibles” 
requiring the most time to. master. Detailed individual session 
results and accompanying evaluations are maintained in unit training 
files and are available to those who require access.
