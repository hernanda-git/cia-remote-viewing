2. S/SK/WNINTEL) The SUN STREAK office uses three different methods of Remote 
Viewing (RV): 


a. (S/SK/WNINTEL) Coordinate Remote Viewing (CRV) 
(1) Site acquisition by coordinate designation 
(2) Monitor present during viewing 
(3) Highly structured reporting format 
(4) "Alpha/Beta State" environment 
(5) Trainable 
b. (S/SK/WNINTEL) Extended Remote Viewing (ERV) 
(1) Variety of site acquisition techniques 
(2) Interviewer present during viewing 
(3) Primarily~ verbal reporting combined with sketching when appropriate 
(4) "Delta/Beta State" environment 
(5) Trainable 
c. (S/SK/WNINTEL) Spontaneous Remote Viewing (SRV) 
(1) Site acquisition by sealed envelope 
(2) Monitor/Interviewer not present during viewing 
(3) Reporting by written summary of perceptions 
(4) Viewer's choice of working environment 


(5) Not trainable - used only with experienced viewers
