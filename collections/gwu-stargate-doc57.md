# GWU National Security Archive: STARGATE Document 57

From the George Washington University National Security Archive, Electronic Briefing Book 438.

Document 57 is the CIA program history overview: "Star Gate is a Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA)
program which involved the use of paranormal phenomena, primarily remote viewing, for intelligence
collection." The research program was government-sponsored, with the following objectives:
operations (using remote viewing against operational targets), research (basic research into the
phenomenon), and training (developing remote viewing capability).
