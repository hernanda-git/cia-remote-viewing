# NSA Psychokinesis and Remote Viewing (SIGINT/COMSEC Threat)

Declassified NSA document: NSA-RDP96X00790R000100030044-7, "Parapsychology, The SIGINT/COMSEC Threat,"
approved for release 2011/12/01.

Summary from the released text:

Remote Viewing: Laboratory demonstrations have shown that gifted individuals using remote viewing
can describe small details in a room or describe a SIGINT site and particular types of antennae.
These capabilities threaten to (1) uncover covert sites and their targeting capability, and
(2) collect intelligence.

The document reviews Soviet parapsychological research, including Vasilyev's work on telepathy,
and addresses psychokinesis as a potential threat to COMSEC (communications security). It notes
that Soviet research was conducted with and without government sponsorship and provided an
effective cover for broader programs.
